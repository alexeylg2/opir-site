Changes to the app

Journal

What is new in the app, what has changed and what is fixed. Opir is not released yet, so for now this is a journal of its making, day by day. After the first release the entries will go by version.

21 November 2026
  • NewA Copy Client Configuration button in Settings, under External AI clients: it puts ready MCP settings with the exact path to the app on the clipboard.
  • NewThe opir command for MCP clients now ships inside the app: nothing to install separately.
  • NewMCP clients connected at the same time learn of each other’s edits.
  • NewUpdates: Opir → Check for Updates… and a daily check, which the app asks about on its second launch.
  • ChangedThe second set of symbol forms is called US in the interface, not IEC: these are the American forms, with the zigzag resistor.
  • ChangedA switchboard’s sheet draws three times faster, and the check runs twice as fast.
  • ChangedThe decimal mark in millimetres and coordinates follows the interface language, not the system’s region. The assistant names menu commands in the interface language.
  • ChangedThe start window and a document’s window appear and go at once, without the system’s animation.
  • FixedA net line between two pins of one part goes round it instead of hiding in its drawing.
  • FixedA digitised drawing keeps clear of the title block, and the picture under it is clipped to the sheet.
  • FixedIn the English interface the app menu is called Opir. The assistant’s steps show names, not internal identifiers.
20 November 2026
  • ChangedGround and Supply are two separate toolbar buttons again. The kind of ground is chosen in the sign’s properties.
  • FixedThe tooltips of the toolbar buttons are back.
  • FixedThe name of a turned supply sign no longer runs over the sign.
19 November 2026
  • NewLibrary: the first batch of semiconductors — diode and LED sets, Darlington pairs, MOSFETs with their body diode.
  • NewRoute Net is in the Schematic menu and on ⌥⌘R: the net picked is routed with wires.
  • NewThe texture of the desk under the sheet is a choice in Settings: none, grain, paper, felt or canvas.
  • ChangedNew interface colours: a graphite window header, violet for what is selected, phosphor for the assistant’s edits.
  • ChangedEvery document opens in a window of its own; there are no tabs any more.
  • ChangedA new document opens on the assistant, which asks what circuit to build.
  • FixedA net picked in the list comes into view whole. A single sheet can be printed alone.
18 November 2026
  • NewLibrary: the first batch of passive parts. A resistor’s power is marked inside its body.
  • ChangedA net has one name: several names on one net are reported by the check.
  • ChangedThe wire picked is darker than the rest of its net.
17 November 2026
  • NewA wire can be given a colour.
  • NewAn outgoing line: an arrow where a line leaves the schematic.
  • NewA tap of Space shows or hides the check of the schematic.
  • ChangedWhat is selected glows: a halo lies under its lines, and a net picked is lit whole.
  • ChangedThe assistant knows what you changed on the schematic between its answers.
  • FixedA name written on a wire goes with the wire.
16 November 2026
  • NewSwitchboards: the library has breakers, switch-disconnectors, residual-current devices, contactors, relays, surge protection, meters and transfer switches.
  • NewA wire has a section and a material, written beside the line.
  • NewA breaker’s line and the table of groups. A board’s lines are checked against PUE:2026, the Ukrainian wiring rules.
  • NewA bus, the line of group connection of GOST 2.721. A wire enters it with a 45° break.
  • NewThe assistant builds switchboard schematics, and the check looks at a board as a whole.
13 November 2026
  • NewA datasheet inside the document: drop a PDF on a part. The assistant reads it and goes by it.
  • NewThe assistant can stand in a window of its own.
  • NewSymbol editor: a new symbol in the form of another, a grid step, zoom and turning. In drawing: arrows, ovals and rounded corners.
  • ChangedThe assistant makes up no symbols: if the library lacks one, it stops and says what is missing.
10 November 2026
  • NewThe app is named Opir.
  • NewThe interface in Ukrainian. The language is chosen in Settings.
  • NewFind on the schematic (⌘F), PNG export, several parts edited at once.
  • ChangedA net is drawn whole on its sheet, and the list of nets follows the sheet.

The first release

In the works

Opir 1.0

The first public release. What it will have:

  • A schematic editor: a library of GOST and US symbols, wires, buses, labels, ports between sheets.
  • Sheets to ESKD: a frame, a title block, your own forms; the parts list compiles itself.
  • A schematic check that takes you to the place of the error.
  • Switchboards: breakers, RCDs, relays, bars; wire sections and colours; a table of groups with currents and voltage drop.
  • A built-in assistant: a circuit from a description, digitising a photo, checking. Cloud and local models.
  • MCP for Claude Desktop and Claude Code.
  • Export to PDF, SVG, PNG; a netlist for KiCad and SPICE; a bill of materials in CSV.
  • Your own components with a symbol editor; a datasheet kept inside the document.
  • The interface in Ukrainian, Russian and English.
