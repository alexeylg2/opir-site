Privacy

What Opir knows about you

In short: nothing it does not need. The app has no ads, no trackers and collects no statistics.

What stays on your computer

  • Documents. Schematics are .opir files wherever you saved them. Attached datasheets live inside the document.
  • Conversations with the assistant. Kept apart from the document, in the app’s folder on your Mac.
  • The API key. Kept in the macOS Keychain.
  • Your library. Your own components and sheet forms — in the app’s folder.

What is sent, and where

Only when you ask the assistant — and only to the model you chose.

The request carries your message, a description of the schematic (parts, nets, check results) and, if that is turned on in Settings, a picture of the sheet. If the assistant reads a datasheet, its pages go to the model too.

  • An online service — your choice of Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI or a service compatible with the OpenAI API: the data goes to that service’s servers and is handled under its terms. The key and the plan are yours.
  • Your permission first. Before the first request to an online service Opir asks for permission and names what will go: your messages, the schematic, sheet images, attached pictures and datasheets. You can take it back in Settings → Assistant → Sending to the service → Revoke.
  • A local model (LM Studio, Ollama): the request never leaves your computer.
  • Without the assistant the app sends nothing anywhere.

MCP

Access for outside clients is off by default. When on, the server listens on 127.0.0.1 only. What a client does with the schematic next is up to the client and its model.

Updates

A copy downloaded from opir.dev asks for the list of versions at https://opir.dev/appcast.xml once a day — if you agreed to that on the second launch — and downloads a new version from GitHub Releases (github.com/alexeylg2/opir-site). Nothing but an ordinary network request is sent. A copy from the Mac App Store is updated by the App Store itself: Opir contacts nothing for that.

This site

The site is static: no cookies, no trackers, no third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub, like any host, sees the technical details of a request, the IP address among them.
